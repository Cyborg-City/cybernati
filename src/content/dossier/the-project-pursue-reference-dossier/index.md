# Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters (PURSUE)

**PURSUE** (Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters) is an executive initiative launched on May 8, 2026 under a directive from President Donald Trump. Overseen by the Department of War with support from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI), it established a systematic rolling release of declassified UAP-related records held across the federal government.

The Department of War is coordinating efforts across dozens of agencies — including the FBI, NASA, DOE, CIA, and AARO — to identify, review, declassify, and publicly release unresolved UAP records. New materials are posted on a rolling basis as tranches every few weeks.

### Release 01 — May 8, 2026

The first tranche of declassified PURSUE materials. These are unresolved cases — meaning the government is unable to make a definitive determination on the nature of the observed phenomena.

#### Notable Items — Release 01

**FBI Photo Series A (A1–A8)**
A series of declassified FBI field photographs showing anomalous objects captured during investigations. These images were previously redacted or withheld entirely.

**NASA Apollo 12 & 17 Visuals (VM1–VM6)**
Six declassified NASA mission photographs from Apollo 12 (1969) and Apollo 17 (1972) showing unexplained phenomena captured from lunar orbit and surface observations. Astronaut-transcribed logs accompany some of these frames.

#### Notable Items — Release 02

**CIA — Intelligence Information Report: USSR 1973**
[`CIA-UAP-D001_Intelligence_Information_Report_USSR_1973.pdf`](https://archive.org/download/pursue-release-02-documents/CIA-UAP-D001_Intelligence_Information_Report_USSR_1973.pdf)
A declassified CIA intelligence report concerning UAP-related observations in the Soviet Union during 1973.

**DOE — Pantex Image (Amarillo, TX)**
[`DOE-UAP-D001_PANTEX_Image.pdf`](https://archive.org/download/pursue-release-02-documents/DOE-UAP-D001_PANTEX_Image.pdf)
A document related to UAP observations near the Pantex Plant — the primary U.S. nuclear weapon assembly facility near Amarillo, Texas.

**DOE — James Tuck Correspondence**
[`DOE-UAP-D002_JamesTuck_Correspondence.pdf`](https://archive.org/download/pursue-release-02-documents/DOE-UAP-D002_JamesTuck_Correspondence.pdf)
Correspondence involving **James Tuck**, a Manhattan Project physicist later at Los Alamos. Documents early scientific discussions of anomalous aerial phenomena within the nuclear weapons complex.

**DOE — Pajarito Astronomers**
[`DOE-UAP-D003_Pajarito_Astronomers.pdf`](https://archive.org/download/pursue-release-02-documents/DOE-UAP-D003_Pajarito_Astronomers.pdf)
Records from astronomers based at Pajarito Plateau (Los Alamos, NM) — scientists at the intersection of nuclear weapons research and observational astronomy.

**Sandia National Labs — General Correspondence**
[`DOW-UAP-D017_General_Correspondence_Of_Sandia.pdf`](https://archive.org/download/pursue-release-02-documents/DOW-UAP-D017_General_Correspondence_Of_Sandia.pdf)
General correspondence files from Sandia National Laboratories documenting institutional responses to UAP incidents from the engineering and nuclear security perspective.

**ODNI — USPER Narrative (Senior US Intelligence Community)**
[`ODNI-UAP-D001_USPER_Narrative_Senior_USIC.pdf`](https://archive.org/download/pursue-release-02-documents/ODNI-UAP-D001_USPER_Narrative_Senior_USIC.pdf)
A narrative document for senior leadership of the U.S. Intelligence Community via the USPER (UAP Senior Executive Review) process.

**57 Video Captures**
A significant expansion of declassified visual material from DOW sources, nearly doubling the video footage from Release 01. Covers sensor captures from multiple military platforms and theaters.
